## P1. Muse Spark line boxes

Method. Each page image went to Muse Spark (`muse-spark-1.3`, effort `low`) through the Files API (`input_file`, detail high), with one prompt: transcribe every line, give a tight `[x_min, y_min, x_max, y_max]` box on a 0 to 1000 grid. Pages sent:

- TC40 p1: the PDF rendered at 144 dpi (1224×1584).
- TC25 p1: the 1-bit Tobacco800 PNG (2560×3245, white text on black, mean pixel 14 of 255) inverted and resized to 1262×1600 first.
- frt-03 POD: the image-only PDF rendered at 144 dpi (1224×1584).
- TC14 PNG: sent as is (1360×1760).

Gold boxes:

- TC40: every page-1 line of 3 or more words from `pdfplumber` (37 lines, exact).
- frt-03: the four POD relation boxes in `pack.json` (already on the 0 to 1000 grid; checked against its debug image).
- TC25 and TC14: six lines each, drawn by hand from the displayed image and checked on an overlay. Treat them as good to about ±3 grid units.

Scoring. Each gold quote is matched to the model's best line (text ratio at least 0.8). A quote that spans table cells uses the union of same-row lines that cover at least 60% of its words. A **hit** is the matched box's centre falling inside the gold box. IoU is shown beside it.

| Target | Gold lines | Matched | Centre-in-box hits | Mean IoU | Median dy | Median dx |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TC40 p1 (text layer) | 37 | 37 | 8 (22%) | 0.23 | +7.1 (sd 4.3) | -2.4 (sd 49) |
| TC25 p1 (inverted scan) | 6 | 6 | **0 (0%)** | 0.02 | +17.2 (sd 0.9) | -8.3 |
| frt-03 POD (handwriting) | 4 | 4 | 4 (100%) | 0.19 | about +12 (n=4) | mixed |
| TC14 PNG (typed) | 6 | 6 | 5 (83%) | 0.48 | +5.9 (sd 1.4) | -1.2 |
| **Scans pooled** | 16 | 16 | **9 (56%)** | | | |

What the numbers do and do not say:

1. **Reading is fine; position is off.** Every gold line was matched, and the POD handwriting came back right (`09:18`, `10:51`, `N`). The boxes are well shaped but sit lower than the text by a near-constant amount on each page: about one line on TC25 (overlay checked), 6 to 12 grid units elsewhere. Positive dy on all four pages.
2. **The offset is not one constant.** It is +6, +7, +12 and +17 on four pages, so a single global correction is not justified. A diagnostic only: removing each page's median offset lifts TC25 to 6/6 and TC14 to 6/6, and TC40 to 30/37. This was not tested on held-out pages, and I did not find the cause (a resize inside the Files API at detail high is a guess, not a finding).
3. **frt-03's 100% is generous.** Its gold boxes are tall (label plus value, about 28 grid units), so a box one line low still has its centre inside. IoU is 0.10 to 0.31. By the scorer's stricter "union IoU at least 0.5" test the pooled scan result is 3 of 16 (19%), all on TC14.
4. **TC40's text-layer result is not a product risk.** The decision rule says to fix a constant offset on text layers first; the offset is not constant (dy runs from -5 to +15 down the page), and the product never draws a model box on a text-layer page (C16, snap by `pdfplumber`). R-06 owns the exact boxes.
5. One run per page, one prompt, effort `low`. No variance estimate.

Rulings:

- TC25 0%: TC25 leaves the video. The Tobacco800 terms check is still a human task, so this is a second reason. frt-03 is the scan beat, with the dashed box and no zoom on it.
