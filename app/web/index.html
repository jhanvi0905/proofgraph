A coverage check that shows its work.

Give it a claim (“the jewelry theft limit is $1,000”) and the policy documents. It says whether the claim is right, and shows the document, clause and page that decided it. If the documents can’t settle it, it says why instead of guessing.

Run a worked packet Upload your documents

What it will not do: judge wording, answer questions about a date, read scans, or run claims in bulk.

Why it is different

Most document AI sells a better reader. You still re-read the documents to trust its answer.

ProofGraph gives an answer you can check in seconds, or a refusal with its reason.

For the person answering

The controlling document, clause and page in seconds, with a verdict or a named reason.

For the person signing off

Every answer carries its evidence. The file can be defended without re-reading it.

Three answers, and no guessing

Amounts are compared only on the same currency and basis (per loss, per claim, in total). Two rules decide which document controls, and the one used is shown: an endorsement overrides the form it changes; a later effective date overrides an earlier one.

  • The claim is right. The document that controls states the same amount.
  • The claim is wrong. The document that controls states a different amount. Both amounts are shown.
  • This can’t be settled from these documents, with one named reason:
    • documents disagree, and no rule picks one;
    • amounts can’t be compared (say, per loss vs in total);
    • a needed document is missing (an endorsement without its form);
    • no document addresses the question.

Wording claims aren’t judged: the controlling clause is quoted with its page, marked “quoted, not checked”.

It also refuses before running:

  • A scan with no readable text.
  • A document neither the system nor the person checking can identify.
  • A claim it can’t read as one amount or one wording claim.
  • An endorsement without the form it changes: the final terms can’t be known, so it names the missing form.

Every answer can be checked

Every answer is built from quotes: document, page, exact words. Each step is marked read (off the page), derived (by code) or judgment (the model’s or a person’s call), so you know what to check first.

Questions

Is this another chatbot?

No. It checks one claim. The model only quotes; code compares and applies the rules.

What if an answer is wrong?

Every value is boxed on its page. A quote not found exactly on its page is rejected.

Which documents work?

Five sets so far: a 1996 D&O program (SEC filing), ISO homeowners forms, a Chubb cyber policy, US tax statutes and IRS limit notices. On the tax statutes, run as a holdout with answers written first, it got 5 of 12 right, none wrong, and refused 7 with a reason (results). On the Chubb policy it first matched 2 of 5, then 5 of 5 after fixes that followed its output, so that one is not a clean holdout (results).

Is this a coverage opinion?

No. It compares stated amounts and names the controlling document by a stated rule. Not a coverage determination, not legal advice.
