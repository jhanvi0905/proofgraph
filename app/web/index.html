    N-1How it works

    Add the claim and the documents

    Paste the sentence you were asked to trust. Drop in everything it rests on: contracts, amendments, bylaws, memos, even scans.

    Every part is checked against every page

    A claim often says more than one thing. ProofGraph splits it into parts and checks each one against all of your documents, not only the first one that mentions it.

    Each answer comes with its proof

    Each part is marked supported, contradicted, qualified or not in these documents, after weighing exceptions, amendments and dates. Select an answer to open the exact words on their page and check it yourself.

    N-2Beyond a citation

    A citation points at text that agrees with a claim. ProofGraph shows the text that decides it, including the clause that disagrees, which document overrides which, and what the documents do not say.

    • See the clause. An answer that rests on a quote opens it on its page.
    • Know which document wins. Amendments, bylaws and dates are applied, and the override is shown.
    • Trust the silence. When the documents don't say, ProofGraph says so, and says what is missing.

    N-3When the documents don't settle it

    • Not in these documents

      These documents discuss payment terms but never set a late fee.

    • Can't tell

      Two documents clash and nothing decides which one wins. The reason is written beside the answer.

    • Qualified

      The claim holds, but an exception or condition is attached to it.

    N-4Questions

    Is this another chatbot?

    No. You paste a claim, not a question. ProofGraph answers each part of the claim and shows where in the documents it comes from.

    What if an answer is wrong?

    An answer that rests on a quote opens it on its page so you can check it yourself. Quotes that cannot be placed on a page are marked and never decide an answer.

    Which documents work?

    PDF, PNG or JPG, up to 10 files. Pages without a text layer are transcribed by the model and shown with approximate boxes.

    Does it replace my lawyer?

    No. It checks a claim against the documents you give it. It does not give legal advice.
